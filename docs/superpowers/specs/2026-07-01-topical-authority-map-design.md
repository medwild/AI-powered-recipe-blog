## 1. Niche Selection

### Micro-niche: "Easy International Recipes at Home"

**Central Entity**: Easy International Home Cooking
**Entity Type**: Concept / Culinary Tradition
**Definition**: Simplified, accessible versions of traditional recipes from world cuisines, designed for home cooks who want to explore global flavors without professional equipment or hard-to-find ingredients.

### Why This Niche

| Criteria | Assessment |
|---|---|
| SEO Difficulty | Low-Medium (KD 7-24 avg across target clusters) |
| Search Volume | 300K+/mo combined across all clusters |
| Pinterest Potential | HIGH — ethnic/regional cuisine is the most-pinned food category |
| Content Scalability | 50+ countries × 3-5 recipes + technique articles = 200-300+ pieces |
| Competition | Individual food blogs, no dominant media site covers ALL countries |
| Topical Authority Fit | Natural E-A-V structure: Entity=World Home Cooking, Attributes=Countries/Ingredients/Techniques |

### Domain Name — Final Decision

| Domaine | Style | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `augustcook.com` | ✅ Persona-brand — "August" = Chef Augustin + "august" (prestigieux) | **Choisi** |

**Tagline**: "International Home Cooking by Chef Augustin"
**Social handles**: `@augustcook` (Pinterest, Instagram, YouTube)
**Strategy**: Le positionnement "world cuisine" est communiqué par le contenu et la tagline, pas par le domaine. Pattern des blogs à succès : HalfBakedHarvest, PinchOfYum, SmittenKitchen — aucun n'a "world" ou "food" dans le nom.

## 4. Pipeline Adaptation — Persona Strategy

### Decision: Keep Chef Augustin Lefevre

The persona remains **Chef Augustin Lefevre** — a French chef who specializes in making international cuisines accessible to home cooks. This is a stronger brand than switching personas per country:

- **Consistency**: Readers build trust with one voice across all cuisines
- **Credibility**: A French-trained chef exploring world cuisines is a compelling narrative
- **Simplicity**: No need to rebuild the skill/persona system — just update the Strategist prompt

### What Changes

| Component | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Strategist skill | "French cooking recipes only" | "International home cooking — focus on [cuisine] for this batch" |
| Writer skill | "You are Chef Augustin, French cuisine expert" | "You are Chef Augustin, French-trained chef specialized in world cuisines" |
| Pipeline input | `keyword` only | `keyword` + `cuisine` (e.g., "swedish", "finnish") |
| SERP analysis | Generic | Enriched with cuisine-specific entities |

### What Stays

- Chef Augustin persona (name, tone, storytelling style)
- All 6 agents (Strategist, Writer, Auditor, Editor, QA, Image Optimizer)
- 13-step pipeline
- Aor article generation (technique articles per cuisine)
- JSON-LD, SEO metadata, internal linking

### Linking Rules
1. Every spoke links back to its hub
2. Hub links to all its spokes
3. Cross-cluster: Nordic ↔ European (shared entity: rye bread)
4. Outer → Core flow: Basics → Nordic/European (funnels authority inward)
5. No orphan pages

## 5. Traffic Strategy

### Phase 1: Pinterest (Month 1-3)
- Primary traffic source while Google indexes
- 3-5 pins per recipe (hero, step-by-step, infographic)
- Target group boards: "International Recipes", "World Cuisine", "Easy Dinner Ideas"
- Rich Pins enabled for recipe metadata

### Phase 2: Google Organic (Month 3-6)
- Target low-KD keywords first (CSV #3, KD 4-8)
- Build topical authority signal through cluster coverage
- Internal linking circulates PageRank

### Phase 3: Scaling (Month 6-12)
- Expand to new country clusters
- Mediavine/Raptive ad eligibility at 50K sessions/mo
- Recipe schema + FAQ schema for rich snippets
